## The two corrections that matter

### 1. Elena was never the compliance gate. Minors are — and they bind ALL FOUR verticals.

`Res. SPDP-SPD-2025-0028-R` Art. 10.1 obliges a Data Protection Delegate from

> "las instituciones de educación inicial, educación general básica, bachillerato… **o cualquier otra
> institución que trate datos de menores de edad aunque no lo hiciere dentro del ámbito educativo**"

and Art. 10.3 extends it to "**Toda actividad** que conllevare el tratamiento datos personales de
categorías especiales relacionadas con menores de edad". *Toda actividad* reaches a persona natural.

**Consequence: inglés, música and programación trigger the DPD requirement identically to Elena's
vertical, the moment one child enrols.** The MASTER-PLAN sequencing — launch three verticals now,
gate Elena behind compliance — **is incoherent unless the other three are adults-only.**

Two coherent options:
- **(A) Launch inglés / música / programación as adults-only (18+).** The only structure that
  genuinely defers the DPD/DPIA load. Also the segment that fits the free Operador de Capacitación
  route to 0% IVA (below).
- **(B) Enrol no minors at all until the LOPDP block is done.** That is a **2–4 week gate**, not the
  six-month blocker the plan implied.

### 2. Staying outside MINEDUC's authorisation strips the 0% IVA education rate.

Neither investigator caught this. `Reglamento LRTI` Art. 187 confines 0% "**exclusivamente a los
prestados por establecimientos educativos legalmente autorizados**… por el Ministerio de Educación",
and closes verbatim:

> "Los cursos y seminarios ofrecidos por otras instituciones que no sean legalmente reconocidas por el
> Estado como establecimientos educativos, **causarán el impuesto al valor agregado**"

at the general rate — **15% today**.

The very fact that makes this platform licence-free is what costs the 0% rate.

| Students @ $120/mes | Annual revenue | IVA exposure |
|---|---|---|
| 20 | ~$28,800 | **~$4,300/yr** |
| 200 | ~$288,000 | **~$43,000/yr** |

**This dwarfs the entire compliance budget.** Two mitigations:

1. **RIMPE Negocio Popular** — under $20,000/yr, issue notas de venta, no IVA declared at all.
   ⚠️ At $120/mo that ceiling breaks at **~14 students**. It is a very short runway.
2. **Operador de Capacitación** with the Ministerio del Trabajo (which absorbed SETEC) —
   **free**, open to personas naturales, valid 2 years. This is the status that won the CCPPE its 0%
   ruling. Framed around capacitación continua, so it plausibly covers adult inglés/programación/
   música and plausibly does **not** cover children's refuerzo escolar.

**Highest-return action on this entire list: the free Ministerio del Trabajo qualification plus a free
SRI absolución de consulta. Not anything to do with Elena.**

## Elena's actual cost: **$18.12**

She is a *terapista de lenguaje titulada* → campo amplio **09 Salud y bienestar** → she registers her
título with ACESS regardless of who she works for.

- ACESS professional registration: **USD 18.12**, 100% online at `saccs.acess.gob.ec`, portal open
  24/7, only document required is the SENESCYT-registered título. No published SLA — budget 1–3 weeks.
- Scope-of-practice legal opinion: **USD 300–600**. Worth it: `LOS` Art. 198 confines her to her
  título's area, and `Ley Orgánica de Salud Mental` Art. 46 makes the mere **offer** of mental-health
  services by a non-mental-health professional a falta muy grave at 20–40 SBU = **USD 9,640–19,280**.

**The platform itself needs no ACESS permit.** The permit norm (`Res. ACESS-2025-0044-R`) binds only
"los establecimientos y servicios de salud del Sistema Nacional de Salud", and the Norma Técnica de
Telesalud (`AM 00044-2025`) §5 binds only "establecimientos del Sistema Nacional de Salud". The
health-establishment tipología contains **zero** entries for pedagogía, aprendizaje, refuerzo, tutoría
or nivelación — so no permit is required **and none is obtainable**.

## Copy discipline — the largest uncosted exposure

`Ley Orgánica de Salud Mental` Art. 46 prohibits the **offer**, so a sentence in a WhatsApp broadcast
is itself the offence. A parent's LODC complaint ($1,000–4,000 plus forced public rectification) is a
cheaper and likelier attack than anything ACESS would do.

**Banned from the site, WhatsApp scripts, teacher bios and ad copy:**
`terapia` · `diagnóstico` · `tratamiento` · `rehabilitación` · `fonoaudiología` ·
`estimulación temprana` · `consultorio` · `historia clínica` · `paciente`

**Use instead** (MINEDUC's own vocabulary): `refuerzo pedagógico` · `acompañamiento psicopedagógico` ·
`tutorías individuales` · `necesidades educativas específicas` · `estudiante`

**Ship this line on every relevant page:**
> "Umbral ofrece acompañamiento pedagógico. No realizamos diagnósticos clínicos ni terapias de salud."

**Put the banned-terms list in a lint rule over the content files so it cannot regress.**
